# Chapter 12: Future Work & Production Roadmap

## 12.1 Planned Enhancements
1. **Computer Vision Damage Assessment:** Integration of YOLOv8 / Segment Anything Model (SAM) for automated vehicle exterior damage segmentation from accident scene photographs.
2. **Federated Cross-Insurer Fraud Network:** Secure multi-party computation (SMPC) or federated learning to detect organized fraud rings operating across multiple insurance companies without exposing raw customer records.
3. **Conversational Multi-Modal AI Assistant:** Voice and conversational FNOL intake enabling claimants to record damage descriptions in natural language with automated entity parsing.
4. **Blockchain-Backed Document Notarization:** Storing cryptographic document hashes and review decisions onto an enterprise Hyperledger Fabric ledger for tamper-proof judicial admissibility.
